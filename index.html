02 · Program

Six weeks. From Purpose Canvas to live MVP.

AI isn't a topic in this program — it's the power tool in every week. You keep your day job; 4–6 focused hours a week do the rest.

Week 01–02 · Decide & Validate

Purpose, strategy & evidence

  • Purpose Canvas workshop — why you, why this venture
  • ChatGPT & Claude as strategy sparring partners — structured prompts for positioning and market maps
  • 10+ real customer conversations, synthesized with AI
  • A €0 demand test on the real market
A validated venture direction — with proof customers want it.
Week 03–04 · Brand & Model

AI Brand Script & identity

  • AI Brand Script — your venture's story, drafted with AI, sharpened by mentors
  • Visual identity — logo drafts, moodboards & design system with AI tools
  • Pricing & business model, ROI-checked
  • Stakeholder map & internal pitch prep
A brand that looks real — because by then, it is.
Week 05–06 · Build & Launch

Rapid Web MVP & Demo Day

  • Rapid Web MVP — a live product in days, no-code + AI, zero developers
  • Outreach engine: 20 warm conversations launched
  • Pitch rehearsed with brutal, kind feedback
  • Demo Day — and nomination into Mana's Open Incubator for the strongest ventures
A live MVP, a real pitch — and a runway behind it.
