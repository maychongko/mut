01 · Who it's for

You'll recognize yourself in at least one of these.

FoundHer works best for women at the very beginning — idea-stage or almost-registered. Not yet for scaling a seven-figure GmbH.

📝

The Maybe-Someday

The idea has lived in your notes app for two years. You don't need more motivation — you need a deadline, a structure, and eleven women who notice when you don't show up.

💼

The Side-Hustler

You have a job, a payslip, and a quiet feeling there's more. We'll show you how to found nebenberuflich — safely, legally, step by step, without torching your contract.

🌍

The New-in-Germany

New country, new rules. Gewerbe? Freiberufler? Kleinunternehmerregelung? We translate German bureaucracy into plain English — with the forms, in the right order.

02 · Curriculum

Six weeks. One registered, revenue-ready business.

Every week ends with a concrete deliverable. By Demo Day you hold a filed (or filing-ready) registration, a price list, and a pitch you've actually given.

Week 01 — Decide

From idea fog to one idea

  • Skills → idea mapping, the German market lens
  • Kill your three worst ideas (on purpose)
  • Cohort match: your accountability trio
One-idea one-pager you'd bet a Saturday on.
Week 02 — Validate

Evidence, not compliments

  • 10 real customer conversations (scripted)
  • A €0 landing-page demand test
  • Go / pivot / kill — decided with data
A validation folder: what people will pay for, and proof.
Week 03 — Earn

Pricing & the money math

  • Value-based pricing for German customers
  • Revenue math: break-even, runway, side-income
  • Your first offer, written and priced
A price list and one offer you can sell this month.
Week 04 — Register

German paperwork, demystified

  • Gewerbe vs. Freiberufler — which one are you?
  • Kleinunternehmerregelung, taxes, insurance
  • Visa & residence-permit map for non-EU founders
A paperwork pack ready to file — forms filled, in order.
Week 05 — Show up

Brand & first customers

  • Positioning: the one sentence you're known for
  • Simple site + presence that doesn't take months
  • The outreach engine: 20 warm conversations
A launch-ready presence and your first outreach batch sent.
Week 06 — Launch

Pitch & Demo Day

  • Your 3-minute pitch, rehearsed with feedback
  • The 90-day plan after the bootcamp
  • Demo Day: on stage, in front of a real room
A pitch on stage — and a community that answers at 11pm.
03 · Format

A rhythm you can keep — beside a job, kids, and a life.

Part-time by design. Two evenings and one Saturday per week, plus 2–4 focused hours in between. Everything is recorded, everything in English first.

Tue + Thu · 19:00–21:00

Live evening sessions

Teaching + hot-seat coaching on Zoom. Cameras on, egos off. Your questions get answered live — not in a forum, next week.

Saturdays · 10:00–14:00

In-person workshops

Hands-on workdays in Berlin & Düsseldorf (streamed if you're far). This is where ideas stop being theoretical — and where friendships start.

In between

Templates & doing the work

Bilingual templates for everything: interview scripts, invoice, contract, pitch deck. You don't start from a blank page. Ever.

🎥 Life happens — kids get sick, jobs get busy. Every session is recorded, and your trio catches you up. Nobody falls out quietly.
