02 · Program

Six weeks. One validated, funded business case.

Your people keep their day jobs. One evening cohort rhythm plus coaching — and every week ends with evidence your management can actually decide on.

Week 01 — Decide

From idea fog to one venture

  • Map team skills to company strategy
  • Kill weak ideas early — on purpose
  • Align sponsors & success criteria
One venture idea with a named sponsor and a one-pager.
Week 02 — Validate

Evidence, not opinions

  • 10+ real customer conversations (scripted)
  • A €0 demand test on the real market
  • Go / pivot / kill — decided with data
A validation folder: what customers will pay for, with proof.
Week 03 — Model

Pricing & the money math

  • Business model options, priced
  • ROI math your CFO will respect
  • Make-or-buy and cannibalization checks
A priced offer and a business model the numbers support.
Week 04 — Build the case

The corporate paperwork, demystified

  • Business case in your company's format
  • Stakeholder map: who says yes, who blocks
  • Budget, legal, and compliance pre-checks
A business case pack ready for your internal approvals.
Week 05 — Show up

Prototype & internal story

  • A landing page or prototype customers touch
  • The one sentence the venture is known for
  • 20 stakeholder & customer conversations
Something people can see, use, and remember.
Week 06 — Decide

Demo Day with management

  • The 3-minute pitch, rehearsed with feedback
  • Decision ask: pilot, budget, owners
  • The 90-day implementation plan
A yes/no/iterate decision from management — in the room.
